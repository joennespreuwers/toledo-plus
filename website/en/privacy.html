Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short: Toledo+ shows your own Toledo and KU Loket data differently, and that data stays in your browser. No tracking of who you are; just an anonymous daily ping you can turn off.

Which websites Toledo+ uses

Toledo+ only has access to these addresses (the host_permissions in its manifest):

  • toledo.kuleuven.be and ultra.edu.kuleuven.cloud: Toledo and the Blackboard Ultra course pages.
  • webwsp.aps.kuleuven.be: KU Loket, for your timetable, exams and shortcuts.
  • cloud.timeedit.net: the personal timetable feed (iCal) KU Loket gives you, as a fallback when KU Loket itself can’t be reached.
  • icts-p-toledo-valvas.cloud.icts.kuleuven.be: Ad Valvas. When you open an Ad Valvas notice in Toledo+, Toledo+ reads only the page of that one notice (title, sender, recipients, date, attachments and text) with your own Ad Valvas session and shows it. Nothing else is read and nothing is ever sent or changed. The text stays only in your browser’s memory (at most 30 minutes), is not stored, synced or passed on, and images in the notice are not loaded. If Ad Valvas asks you to sign in, Toledo+ opens the notice in a new tab so the normal KU Leuven sign-in happens there; Toledo+ never sees your password.
  • api.toledoplus.be: Toledo+’s own server (a Cloudflare Worker), for three things: the anonymous usage signal, the update check and feedback you send yourself (see below).

When you download a file, your browser fetches it through Toledo’s own signed download links.

Besides storage, Toledo+ asks for one permission: declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess. It is used for one rule: redirecting toledo.kuleuven.be/ to toledo.kuleuven.be/portal/, so you skip the general start page (can be turned off in Settings → Advanced). Nothing is read or blocked.

Your session

Toledo+ uses your existing, signed-in session in your own browser. It never sees or stores your password.

What Toledo+ reads

Exactly what Toledo and KU Loket already show you: your enrolments, notifications, unread mail (the count only), links, course content, announcements, deadlines and teachers’ names and e-mail addresses. From KU Loket: your classes (course, time, room, teacher), your exams and the link to your timetable feed.

From Ultra also your first name, only to greet you; it stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Grades and submissions are never read.

What Toledo+ writes back

Only when you ask for it, and only if Settings → Sync → Mark as read in Toledo is on: marking notifications as read, seen or dismissed (and unread again with “Mark as unread”). This goes to Toledo’s own API. Toledo+ never writes to KU Loket.

Storage on your device

  • Settings (language, appearance, nicknames, colours, your own deadlines …) in chrome.storage.sync. If browser sync is on, your browser syncs them between your own browsers.
  • A cache of Toledo and KU Loket data, visit times and what you ticked off, in chrome.storage.local on this device.
  • The search index for ⌘K: titles, kinds and folder paths of the items in your courses, without file contents. Only in chrome.storage.local, never synced or sent anywhere. Turn it off or rebuild it in Settings → Search.
  • Logging out through Toledo+ (account menu → Log out) immediately deletes what was fetched for you: the Toledo and KU Loket cache, your timetable link, the search index, your first name and recently opened documents. Whoever uses the same browser after you sees nothing of yours. Your settings, own deadlines and what you ticked off stay.

Anonymous usage ping and feedback

By default, Toledo+ sends one anonymous signal a day to https://api.toledoplus.be with exactly four things: a random install id (created on your device, not linked to you), the Toledo+ version, the browser and the interface language. That tells us how many people use Toledo+ and which versions are still running.

  • No name, no e-mail address, no student number and no Toledo or KU Loket data.
  • Your IP address is not stored. To limit abuse the server only keeps a daily counter under a one-way hash of your IP address (for IPv6: its network part) with a secret key that changes every day; those counters are deleted after 2 days.
  • Turn it off in Settings → About → Anonymous usage signal or straight from the welcome card. Then nothing is sent any more, the id is deleted on your device and Toledo+ asks the server to delete everything for that id (if that fails, Toledo+ keeps retrying until it succeeds).

Update check (separate switch in Settings → About, on by default): twice a day Toledo+ asks api.toledoplus.be which version is newest. Nothing about you is sent.

Feedback you send from Toledo+ goes to the same address: your text, your contact details only if you fill them in, and — if you leave the box ticked — the version, browser, language and the page you were on. Your install id is never included. Your text (with version, browser, language and page) may appear as a public issue on GitHub; your contact details never do. So don’t include names, student numbers or course content.

The server runs on Cloudflare Workers and D1 (Cloudflare processes the requests as host); public feedback lives on GitHub.

What Toledo+ does not do

  • No tracking of who you are: no analytics services, crash reporting, ads or third-party trackers.
  • No third-party services, fonts or CDNs. All code ships inside the extension; the only server of its own is api.toledoplus.be for the anonymous ping, the update check and feedback.
  • Your Toledo and KU Loket data is never shared with the developer, KU Leuven, other institutions or anyone else.
  • No remote code: the extension never downloads or executes code.

Deleting your data

Settings → Advanced → Reset everything resets every setting, deletes your own deadlines and everything Toledo+ kept in this browser (cache, KU Loket, search index, file index, install id). Your choice for the usage signal and update check stays. Or remove the extension (chrome://extensions → Remove). On the server only the rows from the table above remain: resetting or turning the signal off asks the server to delete the rows of your install id; for feedback, open a Discussion. If you remove the extension without turning the signal off first, those rows disappear after 400 days.

This website

toledoplus.be is a static website on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. As the host, GitHub may keep technical server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement. The demo runs entirely in your browser with fake data and sends nothing anywhere.
